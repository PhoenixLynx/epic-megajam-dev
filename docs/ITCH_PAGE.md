## Short description (the one-liner under the title)

A first-person workday at HALCYON Continuity, where everyone is a little too happy, the clocks stop at 2:14, and something is directing every take.

## Description

You are Employee 4471. It's 9:00 AM. Your coffee is perfect, your inbox is full, and your compliance training is overdue.

Everything at HALCYON is exactly the way it should be: the smiles, the desks, the mug with your number on it. It was exactly this way yesterday, too.

Get through the day:
- Read your email and finish your training.
- Solve the glyph locks to earn your access passes.
- Throw the stapler (HR is counting).
- Make it to the wrap party.

But when Fear rises, the office stops pretending: the lights stutter, your coworkers stop working to watch you, and something tall starts walking the corridors.

At the end of every take, someone will ask for your keys. **Director's cut, or theatrical cut?**

*Made in 72 hours, solo, for the 2026 Epic AI MegaJam (theme: Director's Cut).*

## How it was made

- Built in Unreal Engine 5.8 with C++: the systems are Fear, the coworkers, the stalker, the glyph locks, the two endings, and a cake you can actually cut.
- Every asset and line of code was made during the jam.
- **Music:** original *Halcyon* themes composed in Ableton during the jam (as SkyYamaha).
- **AI tools, transparently** (the full ledger is in the devlog):
  - **Claude (Anthropic):** dev partner and record-keeper. C++ and Unreal Python, generated textures and props (Blender scripts), level dressing, game text, log and playtest-note review, this devlog.
  - **ChatGPT / Sol (OpenAI):** creative assistant. Character faces, the stalker face, the title key art and itch cover, the first ending-art drafts.
  - Direction, design decisions, playtesting, music and final approval: me.
- **Fonts:** Inter, Poppins, Caveat (SIL Open Font License), Permanent Marker (Apache 2.0).
- Unreal Engine is a trademark of Epic Games. The only logo used is the Unreal Engine logo.
