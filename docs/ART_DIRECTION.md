# Art direction: HALCYON (the bright side) vs Fear (the dark side)

The horror only lands if the office is genuinely **pleasant first**. Every rule below has a Fear-state opposite, driven by the single Fear value.

## HALCYON: the interface (Fear 0)

**Feeling:** a calm, expensive, slightly too-perfect wellness-tech campus. Nothing is out of place. Nothing is dirty. Everyone smiles.

| Element | Bright campus | Fear opposite |
|---------|---------------|---------------|
| **Palette** | Off-white walls, blue-grey carpet, **HALCYON mint** (#7FD3C4) and **sky blue** (#A7C7F2) accents, warm pale wood | Desaturated, green-grey grime (#33382F), wet sheen, red only on warnings |
| **Light** | Bright, even, soft daylight from above. No hard shadows. Slight warm white | Flickering fluorescents, pools of dark, cold blue-white, the letterbox closes in |
| **Plants** | Fake ficus in white pots in every corner, planters in the lobby, succulents on desks. Glossy, never a dead leaf | Same plants, but leaves droop or point toward you; pots sit in water |
| **Signage** | The half-sun HALCYON logo, motivational posters, friendly wayfinding ("Wellness Center →") | Posters change wording; signs point the wrong way; "THERE WAS NO CORRIDOR B" |
| **People** | Faceless mint mannequins, posed naturally at desks and the kitchen | Turned to face you; posed mid-step; closer each time you look |
| **Sound** | Bright ambient, HVAC hush, keyboard clicks, a distant PA chime | Hum detunes, heartbeat, the PA repeats |
| **Order** | Supplies squared on desks, chairs tucked, mugs on coasters | Chairs pulled out, paper on the floor, a mug in the wrong place every rewind |

## Shape language

- **Rounded and soft** for HALCYON things: pill-shaped pass readers, rounded sofa, round café tables, the half-sun logo.
- **Hard, tall and thin** for the malware: the stalker is too tall and too thin, with a dark suit and no face. Doorways in Fear feel taller.
- **Film-set tells** sneak in as the game goes on: tape marks on the floor, a boom-mic shadow, the director's chair, a REC light.

## Rooms at a glance

- **Lobby:** the showroom. Sofa, coffee table, planter, the HALCYON sign, receptionist, guard by the elevator, the Chairman's portrait.
- **Open office:** 12 cubicles, mint panels, supplies on every desk (= **weapons**), your desk #4471 by the west wall.
- **Kitchen:** counter + sink, coffee machine, kettle, microwave, fridge with a memo, mint vending machine, café tables, water cooler. The friendliest room; the first to rot in Act 2.
- **Conference room:** long table, mint meeting chairs, the call screen.
- **Supply closet:** binders and paper reams; Key Lock 2.
- **Corridor B:** bare on purpose (the loop must look identical); the custodian's mop bucket at the entrance.
- **Wellness Center → Hospital:** white, clinical, plants; becomes the ward with curtains, charts and the REC light.

## Throwables (the player's arsenal)

Everything small on a desk or counter: mug (coffee burn), keyboard, stapler, tape dispenser, pen cup, desk lamp, phone, laptop, monitor, PC tower, binders, paper reams, desk plants, kettle, microwave, trash bins, chairs, bar stools, wet-floor signs, mop bucket, fire extinguisher. Heavy items (PC tower, microwave, chairs) hit harder but are slower to throw; cabinets need telekinesis (3 soul bits).
